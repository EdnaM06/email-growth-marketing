# 📧 Customer-email-growth-analytics

## 🧠 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer behavior, campaign response, and purchase patterns to identify opportunities for improving **email targeting, segmentation, and conversion rates**.

The goal is to simulate a real-world marketing analytics problem:
> How can we use customer data to increase campaign effectiveness and revenue?

---

## 🏢 Business Context

Companies like **Ruby Labs** operate subscription-based or digital products where:
- Customer retention is critical
- Email campaigns drive revenue
- Segmentation improves conversion
- Poor targeting leads to revenue loss

This analysis focuses on improving:
- Campaign acceptance rates
- Customer segmentation strategy
- Revenue-driven targeting decisions

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

- Source: Public marketing analytics dataset (iFood-style customer data)
- Customers: 2,206
- Features: 39 columns

### Includes:
- Customer demographics
- Purchase behaviour
- Campaign response history
- Channel usage (web, catalog, store)
- Product spending patterns
